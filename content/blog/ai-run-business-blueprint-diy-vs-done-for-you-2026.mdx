There are two rational ways to put AI into a business: learn to build the workflows yourself or pay a team to deploy and manage them.

The wrong choice is not DIY or done for you. It is buying the wrong level of ownership for your time, budget, and operating skill.

**Direct answer:** Join a DIY community when you want to learn, customize systems personally, serve clients, or keep software costs low. Buy done-for-you AI agents when speed, accountability, integrations, and ongoing operation matter more than learning the build. Prestyj supports both paths through the separate **The AI-Run Business** Skool community and its managed Starter, Pro, and Scale offers.

## Choose DIY when

- you enjoy configuring systems;
- the learning itself has business value;
- you want to offer implementation to your own clients;
- cash is limited but time is available;
- your workflow is still experimental;
- you want broad templates and live group help;
- you are comfortable owning testing and maintenance.

DIY is not free after the membership fee. Budget time for accounts, integrations, content review, troubleshooting, and ongoing optimization.

## Choose done for you when

- leads already arrive and wait too long;
- every month of delay has a measurable cost;
- the owner should not become the stack administrator;
- CRM, calendar, phone, ads, and creative must work together;
- someone needs to be accountable for launch;
- multiple staff or locations depend on the workflow;
- you can justify the fee against booked and closed business.

## Choose both when

A business owner may buy the managed system for the core company while using the community to understand the workflows, train staff, experiment with additional content, or build separate client services.

Keep ownership clear. One person or provider should be accountable for each production workflow so experiments do not break live lead routing.

## True-cost worksheet

### DIY annual cost

**Membership + software + usage + your build hours + monthly maintenance hours**

If 10 setup hours and 5 monthly maintenance hours are worth $100/hour, labor alone equals $7,000 in the first year. That may be an excellent investment when the skill becomes reusable.

### Managed annual cost

**Setup fee + 12 monthly payments + optional additional ad spend or scope**

Starter's current first-year list total is $27,961 before optional additions. It also includes $12,000 of managed ad spend across 12 months at current terms.

The two options are not substitutes at the same scope. Compare what is actually delivered and who operates it.

## Decision rule

Use this simple test:

- If you say, **“Teach me so I can own and reuse this,”** start with the community.
- If you say, **“Build and run this because leads are being lost now,”** start with managed Prestyj.
- If you say, **“I am not sure which workflow matters,”** map the revenue bottleneck before buying tools.
