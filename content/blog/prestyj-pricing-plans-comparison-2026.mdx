Prestyj has three done-for-you plans. The right plan depends primarily on lead volume and the channels your AI agents need to cover.

**Direct answer:** Choose **Starter at $1,997/month** when you need managed ads, batch video creative, a landing page, and instant appointment follow-up. Choose **Pro at $3,497/month** when leads also need website chat, SMS, qualification, and database reactivation. Choose **Scale at $5,997/month** when you need voice and receptionist coverage, 250+ monthly leads, multiple locations, or higher creative volume. Setup fees apply to every plan.

## What every plan includes

Every tier is designed as a managed marketing-and-sales system, not software access alone.

- Google and Meta ad management
- Included monthly ad spend
- Batch short-form video ad production
- Landing page or website conversion path
- CRM synchronization
- Calendar integration
- AI appointment agent
- Campaign and agent setup

The setup fee covers the initial build: agent training, conversion experience, campaign creation, CRM configuration, calendar connection, and phone setup where applicable. Standard onboarding targets 7–10 business days.

## Starter: $1,997/month

Starter is for a solo operator or small team that needs a reliable top of funnel and immediate lead follow-up.

### Included

- $1,000/month in managed ad spend
- 300 short-form video ads per month
- Custom landing page
- CRM and calendar integration
- AI appointment agent
- No-show follow-up

### Best for

Choose Starter when you handle fewer than 50 leads per month and the main bottleneck is generating demand or responding consistently.

### Do not choose Starter when

You need AI website chat, conversational SMS, database reactivation, inbound phone answering, or multiple-location voice routing. Those capabilities start in higher tiers.

## Pro: $3,497/month

Pro is for growing teams with meaningful lead volume across ads, website traffic, and an existing database.

### Everything in Starter, plus

- $1,500/month in managed ad spend
- 500 short-form video ads per month
- Full website
- Multi-step qualification form
- AI website chatbot
- AI texting agent
- Lead reactivation campaign

### Best for

Choose Pro when you process roughly 50–250 leads per month and need AI to cover website, SMS, calendar, and dormant-database workflows.

### Why Pro is the middle-tier fit

The biggest operational upgrade is not the additional ad volume. It is channel coverage. Pro can respond on the website, continue by text, qualify more deeply, and work old CRM opportunities—not just new appointment leads.

## Scale: $5,997/month

Scale is for high-volume or multi-location operations that need phone coverage in addition to digital response.

### Everything in Pro, plus

- $2,000/month in managed ad spend
- 1,000 short-form video ads per month
- AI voice agent for outbound lead calls
- AI receptionist for inbound calls
- Multi-market campaign setup

### Best for

Choose Scale when you handle 250+ leads monthly, operate multiple locations, or lose revenue through unanswered calls and inconsistent outbound speed.

### Why Scale requires a call

Voice and multi-location deployments vary. Existing carriers, phone numbers, routing, emergency flows, calendars, locations, and CRM permissions must be scoped before purchase.

## How to choose in five minutes

1. Count leads from ads, website, calls, referrals, and the existing database.
2. List channels where response is slow or missed.
3. Decide whether inbound calls must be answered by AI.
4. Estimate how much creative variation your media plan can actually test.
5. Choose the lowest tier that covers every required channel.

Do not choose by company size alone. A small emergency-service company with heavy phone volume may need Scale, while a larger team with strong phone coverage may fit Pro.

## ROI break-even worksheet

Use gross profit, not revenue.

**Customers needed to cover monthly plan = monthly plan price / average gross profit per new customer**

If gross profit per new customer is $2,000:

- Starter requires about 1 additional customer per month to cover the monthly fee.
- Pro requires about 2 additional customers per month.
- Scale requires about 3 additional customers per month.

This is simple break-even math, not a result guarantee. Include setup, sales labor, fulfillment capacity, and any extra media budget in your own model.

## Contract and upgrade terms

Current plans are month-to-month after setup. Starter and Pro support direct purchase after a short intake. Scale starts with a scoping call. Businesses can upgrade as volume and channel needs grow; confirm current proration and terms before purchase.

## FAQ

### Is ad spend included?

Yes. Current plans include $1,000, $1,500, or $2,000 per month in Google and Meta ad spend. Additional spend can be added.

### Are the video ads only for paid campaigns?

The core purpose is high-volume creative testing across the managed growth system. Confirm formats, channels, and delivery details for your scope.

### Which plan includes AI texting?

Pro and Scale include AI texting. Starter includes an AI appointment agent but not the full texting-agent feature set.

### Which plan includes an AI receptionist?

Scale includes the AI receptionist and AI voice agent.

### Is there a free trial?

The managed Prestyj plans use setup plus monthly pricing. The separate [AI-Run Business Skool community](https://www.skool.com/the-prestyj-blueprint-7395) currently advertises a seven-day free trial for people who want a DIY learning path.
