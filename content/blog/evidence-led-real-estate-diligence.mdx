A diligence conclusion is only as useful as the path back to the evidence.

A deal team may review a CIM, leases, operating statements, debt documents, environmental reports, sponsor materials, public records, and the underwriting model. Summarizing those files is not the hard part. The hard part is keeping every conclusion connected to the right source and surfacing disagreement before it reaches committee.

## Build the evidence spine first

For every extracted fact, preserve:

- source document and stable location;
- document date and reporting period;
- entity and property identity;
- extraction method and confidence;
- reviewer status;
- later corrections or superseding evidence.

A paragraph in an IC memo should be able to point back through that chain.

## Compare against the acquisition box

An agent becomes useful when it can compare extracted facts with the firm's own criteria. That may include geography, basis, yield, occupancy, lease concentration, sponsor history, debt structure, or capital requirements.

The criteria must be explicit and versioned. A hidden prompt is not an investment policy.

## Surface conflict, not consensus

The CIM, rent roll, model, and third-party report may describe the same fact differently. The agent should not manufacture one clean answer. It should show the competing values, source dates, and materiality.

## Separate screening from judgment

Public-record and counterparty screening can gather evidence and route potential matches. A named reviewer still determines whether a record belongs to the party and whether the finding matters to the deal.

## Deliver committee-ready work

The finished package can include an abstraction table, open-item log, exception report, draft IC memo, and source packet. Each conclusion should carry enough evidence that the reviewer can challenge it without reopening the entire data room.

That is the difference between document chat and controlled diligence.

## A source register the committee can inspect

Assign each source a stable identifier before extracting conclusions. Use document version and page or section, not a temporary download URL alone. A reviewer with permission should be able to reopen the exact evidence. Access restrictions continue to apply to the register and the source packet.

| --------------------- | ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |

The example identifiers and facts are invented. Do not put confidential source URLs in a public memo or unrestricted AI service. A source-attached conclusion still needs an authorized audience.

## Diligence checklist from intake to committee

- Confirm the acquisition criteria and their version before screening. Record criteria that are unknown rather than inferring the investment team's appetite.
- Inventory available and missing files by asset, entity and reporting period. An empty folder is not evidence that no obligation exists.
- Separate executed agreements, drafts, marketing materials and third-party reports. Each can inform the analysis, but they do not carry the same authority.
- Match lease and tenant identities across the rent roll, abstract and model. Route uncertain matches for review before aggregating.
- Preserve conflicting figures side by side, including their definitions and dates. Calculate the decision impact only under clearly stated assumptions.
- Attach supporting and contrary evidence to each material memo statement. Do not cite a document that merely mentions the topic.
- Assign every open item an owner, requested evidence and committee treatment: resolved, explicit condition, or decision deferred.
- Have the deal lead review the actual memo version and verify that the source packet is accessible to the intended committee.

## Synthetic committee evidence example

A fictional rent roll reports monthly contractual rent of $100,000. An executed amendment would add $4,000 from October, while an underwriting model includes that increase in September. The committee question is not which file looks newer; it is which period and definition the memo describes.

A source-attached memo could say: “September contractual rent remains $100,000 under the reviewed documents. The model includes a $4,000 increase one month early. The deal team has requested a corrected timing assumption; the October step remains subject to verification that no later amendment supersedes the reviewed copy.”

Attach the rent-roll row, amendment clause and model cell. This wording does not conclude that the entire property's underwriting is wrong. It identifies one timing issue and its next action. If committee proceeds with a condition, record that condition and the approving person rather than converting the open item to resolved.

## What not to delegate

An agent may locate a potential counterparty record, but a person must resolve identity, relevance and any legal restrictions on using it. It may summarize an environmental report, but it cannot replace the qualified expert responsible for interpreting the findings. It may prepare a risk paragraph, but investment judgment remains with the authorized decision-maker.

Measure usefulness by whether a reviewer can challenge a conclusion from its attached record. Counting summarized pages alone rewards volume without establishing decision quality.
