Answering the phone is not the outcome. The outcome is a broker receiving the right inquiry, with enough context to act, while the prospect is still moving.

## Define the qualification record

A commercial inquiry may need property, market, use, size, timing, budget, representation status, financing, and next-step availability. The exact fields depend on the assignment.

A voice agent should qualify against those approved criteria, not improvise a generic sales script.

## Preserve consent and disclosure

The caller should know they are interacting with an automated agent. Recording and follow-up must follow the consent and communications rules that apply to the call. The system should record the consent state with the interaction.

## Route on business rules

Routing can depend on property, geography, assignment team, language, urgency, or transaction type. If a rule is ambiguous or the caller requests a person, the agent should escalate instead of forcing completion.

## Hand back the complete record

The broker should receive:

- caller identity and contact details;
- the qualification fields collected;
- the full transcript or structured call notes;
- recording-consent status where applicable;
- classification and urgency;
- scheduled next step;
- unanswered questions;
- the reason for the routing decision.

A phone number without context only moves the coordination burden downstream.

## Test failure paths

Test poor audio, interruptions, missing consent, duplicate CRM records, unavailable calendars, unsupported questions, and requests for a human. The agent should fail closed and preserve the caller's progress.

## Measure the handoff

Useful measures include answered inquiries, completed qualification, correct routing, scheduled next steps, broker acceptance, and exceptions requiring review. A fast response is valuable only when the handoff remains accurate.

## A synthetic broker handoff

The following record is fictional. It contains no real caller details and does not claim that a calendar or CRM integration has been delivered.

| Field             | Illustrative handoff                                               |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Inquiry           | Occupier seeking approximately 8,000 square feet of office space   |
| Market and use    | Named assignment market; general office use stated by caller       |
| Timing            | Target move within six months; exact date unconfirmed              |
| Budget            | Caller declined to provide; do not infer                           |
| Representation    | Caller says another broker is advising; needs human review         |
| Property interest | Asked about Example Building A; availability not verified          |
| Consent state     | Automated-agent disclosure delivered; recording declined           |
| Next step         | Human callback requested; no appointment confirmed                 |
| Routing reason    | Existing-representation question and request for a person          |
| Open questions    | Assignment conflict, approved availability and permitted follow-up |

Because recording was declined, this example should not imply that a recording or full transcript exists. If the approved local policy permits minimal structured notes, keep only what that policy allows. If it does not, route without retaining conversation content. The handoff must reflect what actually happened, including absent fields.

## Qualification should reduce ambiguity, not manufacture certainty

Use approved questions about the commercial requirement: property or market, transaction type, intended use, approximate size, timing, representation and preferred next step. Ask only for information needed to route the inquiry. Do not collect financial account details or sensitive personal information merely because a script has room for them.

Preserve the distinction between caller statements and verified property facts. “Caller seeks 8,000 square feet” is an intake fact. “The property has 8,000 square feet available” requires an approved current listing source. If that source is unavailable, the agent should say that the broker will confirm rather than invent availability or terms.

## Escalation conditions

Route to a person when the caller asks, when representation or assignment rules conflict, when the agent cannot understand a material detail, or when a question goes beyond the approved property information. Urgent safety concerns should follow the firm's emergency guidance, not remain in a qualification script.

If the designated broker is unavailable, preserve the permitted callback request and show its status as awaiting acceptance. Do not mark the lead handled because a routing event fired. A confirmed appointment requires a successful calendar response and the necessary participant agreement, not a model's statement that it booked one.

## Context-dependent consent review

Recording, automated-agent disclosures, follow-up calls, texts and email can raise different requirements. Relevant facts include the parties' locations, inbound versus outbound contact, purpose, channel, recording or transcription behavior, and any vendor processing. Qualified counsel should review the actual call flow and jurisdictions before launch.

Do not treat consent to one call as blanket consent to marketing across channels. Store the scope and timing of a permission or refusal where the approved policy requires it. Honor revocation and suppression rules. If the legal or operational state is unknown, stop the affected recording or follow-up action and offer a human path.

These are review questions, not a statement that one script satisfies every jurisdiction. A disclosure alone does not establish permission to retain or reuse a voice recording.

## Acceptance checklist

- Replay synthetic cases with poor audio, interruption and a request for a person.
- Verify that declined or unknown recording permission follows the approved non-recording path.
- Check duplicate-record handling and the difference between a draft CRM note and a confirmed update.
- Test stale listing information and calendar outages without making unsupported promises.
- Have brokers rate whether the handoff supports a next action, and include rejected handoffs in the denominator.
- Restrict transcript or note access and retention to the approved business purpose.
