**Subject:** Rate-card audit: the $0.05 AI answering quote is not an all-in call cost

Hi ACHR News team,

I reviewed the public rate cards for four voice-AI vendors on July 31, 2026. The useful HVAC buyer angle is not which vendor is cheapest; it is why the advertised units are not directly comparable:

- **Vapi:** $0.05 per minute for the platform layer, with model, voice, speech recognition, transport, and telephony selected separately.
- **Retell:** $0.07–$0.31 per minute pay as you go, with the final rate changing by model, voice, telephony, and add-ons.
- **Bland:** $0.11–$0.14 per connected minute across self-serve tiers, plus $0–$499 monthly plan fees and separate transfer-time rates.
- **Synthflow:** enterprise contracts start at $30,000 annually, with final scope based on volume, concurrency, telephony, integrations, security, and launch support.

At 5,000 connected minutes, the published arithmetic is $250 for Vapi’s platform layer alone, $350–$1,550 across Retell’s stated range, and $700–$1,049 across Bland’s self-serve tiers before optional extras. Those totals still do not normalize implementation, failed-call review, after-hours escalation, transfer behavior, or human QA.

For HVAC operators, a practical quote worksheet would compare the same inbound workflow and report fully loaded cost per resolved call or booked qualified appointment—not headline cost per minute.
