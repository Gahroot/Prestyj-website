**Subject:** Story idea: AI avatar ads give real estate teams the wrong denominator

Hi HousingWire team,

Real estate teams comparing AI avatars with agent-led video are usually shown production metrics—subscription price, render speed, and number of clips. None answers the buyer question: **which format produces more qualified appointments from the same offer and media budget?**

A useful service piece for HousingWire readers would replace the “AI avatar vs human” opinion fight with a controlled 25-ad test:

- 10 avatar ads and 10 real-face ads using the same five hooks
- 5 neutral controls to reveal when both spokesperson formats lose to a clearer concept
- one audience, offer, landing page, budget, and attribution window
- cost per qualified appointment and show rate as the decision metrics—not clicks
