**Subject:** 2026 solar PPC trap: the federal homeowner credit ended, but old ad claims did not

Hi Search Engine Land editors,

A practical paid-search compliance story is hiding in a date change: the IRS now says the Residential Clean Energy Credit is unavailable for property placed in service after December 31, 2025.

That makes a common evergreen solar claim — “get the 30% federal tax credit” — inaccurate for a homeowner system placed in service in 2026. The risk is bigger than one ad: the old language can persist in responsive-search-ad assets, landing-page FAQs, call scripts, structured data, comparison tables, and remarketing creative.

A useful marketer checklist would be:

1. Remove generic 30% federal homeowner-credit claims from 2026 residential campaigns.
2. Separate homeowner-owned residential projects from commercial or third-party-owned structures; do not transfer eligibility language between them.
3. Name the exact state or utility program instead of saying “solar incentives.”
4. Put the source URL, geography, eligibility conditions, and verification date in the campaign brief.
5. Recheck the claim before relaunching seasonal creative or cloning an older campaign.
