## Sending routine and follow-up

The distribution owner aims for **three relevant editorial pitches per week**, not three guaranteed placements. First verify each outlet's current editorial fit, submission rules and appropriate public channel. The subject-matter owner clears claims; the release owner verifies the linked version; the sender confirms identity and any applicable communications obligations. No bulk automation or purchased contact list is part of this program.

Record sent date only after an actual send, reply date only after a reply, and link date only after verifying the published referring URL and target. One courteous follow-up after seven business days is the default if the channel's rules allow it. Stop after a decline, opt-out or a second unanswered approach; do not keep cycling the same pitch. Review relevance weekly and prefer a better-fit new angle over repeated nudges.

If all current content is awaiting review, the week's result is three prepared pitches and zero sends, not three outreach completions. Keep production, distribution and earned-link states separate in `data/seo/institutional-outreach.json`.
