# Institutional real estate domain

Write for investment, fund accounting, investor relations, asset management, portfolio operations and commercial brokerage teams. Do not impersonate a qualified professional or invent years of experience.

Useful artifacts include diligence exception logs, source-attached committee drafts, reconciled close schedules, scoped investor answers, portfolio variance packs and accepted broker handoffs. State the entity, period, source version and reviewer. Distinguish actuals, forecasts, contractual terms and assumptions.

A lease abstract is not an executed agreement. A newer upload is not necessarily a newer effective term. A draft answer is not an approved report. A routed inquiry is not an accepted handoff. A prepared distribution calculation is not payment authorization.

Do not apply residential brokerage rules to commercial work without verified relevance. Recording, calling, messaging, investor disclosures and financial interpretations require context-specific qualified review. Do not state universal consent or compliance guarantees.

Geo/niche/comparison mass generation is inactive. Do not propose residential lead funnels, contractor content, batch-ad subscription plans or retired comparison pages. Every example must support a distinct institutional reader task, not a keyword permutation.
